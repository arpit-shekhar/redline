FREELANCE DESIGN AGREEMENT

This agreement is between Northgate Bakery Ltd ("the Client") and the designer named below ("the Designer"). It is a test document written for Redline and describes no real parties.

1. Services. The Designer will produce a logo and a set of packaging labels for the Client's new range of bread.

2. Fees. The Client will pay the Designer a fixed fee of 2,400 pounds. Payment is due within 60 days of the Client's acceptance of the final designs.

3. Acceptance. The Client may request up to three rounds of revisions. The final designs are accepted when the Client confirms acceptance in writing.

4. Ownership. All rights in the designs pass to the Client on delivery, whether or not payment has been made.

5. Term. This agreement renews automatically for further periods of twelve months unless either party gives 90 days' written notice before the renewal date.

6. Termination. The Client may end this agreement at any time by giving 7 days' written notice. The Designer may end this agreement by giving 60 days' written notice.
